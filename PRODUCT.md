# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Salon, barbershop, and beauty/aesthetics business owners and their front-desk staff (receptionists/attendants), using the same panel with no dominant persona. Owners check dashboards, finance, and reports in shorter, lower-frequency sessions. Staff use it operationally all day: booking appointments, registering customers, checking schedules between clients — often on a shared front-desk screen, sometimes with a customer standing nearby. Interruptions are frequent; clarity and speed matter more than novelty.

## Product Purpose

AgendaPro is scheduling and business-management software for beauty/personal-care businesses: appointments, customers, services, employees, and finance in one panel. Success is a staff member completing a booking or lookup in seconds without hunting for the right control, and an owner trusting the numbers on the dashboard at a glance.

## Brand Personality

Modern, precise, calm confidence — inspired by Linear, Stripe, and Vercel. Minimal chrome, purposeful color, restrained motion. Should feel like professional B2B software a business owner would be proud to have open on their counter, not a generic admin template. Blue/indigo as the single accent carrying brand identity; everything else is quiet tinted neutrals.

## Anti-references

- Generic Bootstrap/admin-template look: boxy cards, rainbow chart palettes, heavy drop shadows, default form styling.
- Cluttered dashboards with too many competing widgets and no hierarchy.
- Consumer-beauty styling (pastel gradients, script fonts, decorative flourishes) — this is B2B operational software, not a consumer beauty app.
- Gradient text, glassmorphism-as-decoration, side-stripe card borders, identical icon+heading+text card grids — standard AI-slop tells, avoid regardless of trend.

## Design Principles

- Operational clarity first: staff scan and act under interruption, so hierarchy, contrast, and click targets must survive a distracted 3-second glance.
- One accent, used deliberately: blue/indigo signals primary actions and active state — never decorative.
- Quiet by default, precise on demand: restrained surfaces and spacing; density and detail (badges, hover states, table affordances) earn their place through use, not decoration.
- Consistency over novelty: the same card, table, and form patterns repeat across every module (dashboard, customers, services, employees, appointments, finance) so nothing has to be relearned.
- Never regress functionality: every redesign change is additive to the visual layer only — business logic, data, and routes are untouched.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA: body text ≥4.5:1 contrast (including in dark mode and on tinted surfaces), large text ≥3:1, visible keyboard focus states on all interactive elements, no color-only signaling (status/badges pair color with icon or label), respects `prefers-reduced-motion`.
